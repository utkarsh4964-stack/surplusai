🌱 The Rescue Journey

From surplus plate to a full stomach

01

🍱 Donate

A restaurant, hotel or kitchen shares its surplus meals before they ever become waste.

02

👁 Care Check

Our Quality & Expiry agents gently verify freshness and the safe window for consumption.

03

🎯 Match

The Matching agent finds the NGO with the greatest need, closest by, ready to receive.

04

🚲 Deliver

A nearby volunteer is assigned instantly, with a clear route and honest ETA.

05

🌍 Impact

Every rescued meal becomes a real, countable act — people fed, waste and emissions avoided.
