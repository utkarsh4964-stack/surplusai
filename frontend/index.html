04

🚲 Deliver

🏠 NGO Partner Portal

Meals headed your way

This is your organisation's window into the rescue network. SurplusAI plugs you directly into a live feed of surplus meals from nearby restaurants, hotels and bakeries — no phone calls, no chasing drivers, no guessing what's coming. The Matching Agent only sends donations that fit your need, your capacity and your location, and every one arrives pre-screened for freshness before a volunteer is even assigned.

Before the volunteer arrives

  • Keep a staff member near the gate around the pickup window
  • Have a cooler or steel container ready for hot food
  • Confirm today's headcount so nothing is over- or under-served
  • Flag any issue on arrival — it feeds straight back to the Quality Agent
