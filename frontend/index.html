🏠 NGO Partner Portal

Meals headed your way

SurplusAI partners with verified NGOs, shelters, orphanages and community kitchens across the city. Once you're onboarded, our Matching Agent treats your organisation as a live node in the rescue network — weighing your stated need, capacity and proximity every time a new donation comes in, so meals land where they'll do the most good, not just wherever's closest.

🎯

How you get matched

Your profile's need level, remaining capacity and distance from the donor are scored together — you're never just "next in line."

🔔

What you'll receive

A confirmed donation with food type, quantity, pickup window and an assigned volunteer's ETA — no back-and-forth calls needed.

✅

Freshness, guaranteed

Every donation already passed our Quality & Expiry agents before it's routed to you, so you always know it's safe to serve.

📈

Your impact, tracked

Meals received, people fed and waste diverted through your organisation all roll up into the network-wide Impact Hub.

📋 Partner Responsibilities

  • Confirm pickup within the window shared in your notification.
  • Have someone available to receive the volunteer at the door.
  • Report any quality concerns immediately so the Quality Agent can learn from it.
  • Keep your capacity and need level updated so future matches stay accurate.

🔔 Incoming Donations

Read-only view for our NGO partners. Every donation the Matching Agent routes to you appears here the moment it's confirmed, sourced from the same live pipeline as the Donor Portal.

🚲 Volunteer Portal

Your next delivery run

Thank you for showing up for your community. Every run you take on closes the loop between a restaurant's surplus and a family's next meal — the Route Agent assigns you the nearest active donation, calculates a realistic ETA from live traffic conditions, and hands you a clear pickup-to-drop-off path, so you're never guessing.

📍

How you're assigned

The Route Agent picks the closest available volunteer to both the donor and the matched NGO, to keep total transit time as short as possible.

🗺️

Your route, mapped

Pickup point, drop-off point and a live ETA are calculated the moment a match is made — visible to you and to the receiving NGO.

🛡️

Safety & support

Every donation has already cleared a freshness check, and dispatch support is one tap away if a pickup or drop-off doesn't go to plan.

🌍

Your impact

Every completed run adds directly to meals rescued, people fed and CO₂ avoided on the Impact Hub — your effort is always counted.

📋 Before Every Run

  • Confirm the pickup window with the donor before heading out.
  • Handle food containers gently to keep the Quality Agent's freshness check valid.
  • Mark the run complete as soon as it's handed off, so the NGO and Impact Hub update instantly.
  • Flag any delay early — the receiving NGO can see your live ETA either way.
